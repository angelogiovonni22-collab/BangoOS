# Product Identity

## Official Product Definition

**Approved:** Version 1.0

B.O.S. (Bango Operating System) is **the operating system that runs construction companies**.

B.O.S. is not a collection of disconnected business applications.

It is one unified operating system that enables construction companies to plan, operate, monitor, and improve every aspect of their business from a single platform.

Every module within B.O.S.—including CRM, Projects, Finance, Estimating, Scheduling, Equipment, Safety, Payroll, Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable, Documents, Reporting, and Orion—exists as part of one integrated operating system.

Our objective is not to build better construction software.

Our objective is to redefine how construction companies operate.

---

## What This Means

Every product decision should support this vision.

When evaluating a new feature, ask:

> Does this strengthen B.O.S. as the operating system that runs construction companies?

If the answer is no, reconsider the feature before building it.

---

## Product Promise

B.O.S. should help construction companies:

- Operate from one platform.
- Make better decisions.
- Reduce operational complexity.
- Increase visibility across the business.
- Preserve organizational knowledge.
- Improve profitability.
- Enable confident executive leadership.

---

## Long-Term Vision

A construction company should be able to start the workday by opening one application:

**B.O.S.**

Everything required to run the business should be available from that single operating system.
